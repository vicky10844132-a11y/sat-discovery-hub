Digital Twin Workspace

This workspace is the canonical resource-state view for spacecraft, constellations, payloads, ground assets and AOIs.

  • Select any object from the resource graph or scene to inspect state.
  • Layer controls hide or restore orbit, asset, ground and AOI overlays.
  • Scene modes switch between operational 3D, 2D and graph-oriented views.
  • Prototype telemetry is clearly presented as workspace state, not as a claim of a connected external provider.
